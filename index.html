Hover Cover — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026
Extension: Hover Cover (Chrome extension), version 1.4.0

Hover Cover shows a cover of your choice over the current browser tab. When you move your pointer into a hover area that you define, the webpage is revealed; when you move the pointer out of the area, the cover is displayed again. This policy explains what the extension does with data. In short: everything stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

  • Your cover image. If you choose the "Custom image" option, you may drag and drop or select one image file (PNG, JPEG, or WebP). The image is processed locally in your browser and one cover image is stored locally on your device so the extension can display it. You can replace it at any time by dropping or selecting a new image; the new image overwrites the old one.
  • Your preferences. Your cover choice (simulated spreadsheet, simulated terminal, or custom image) and your hover-area settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Enabled-tab state. Whether Hover Cover is turned on for a tab is kept in browser session storage and is cleared when the browser session ends.

Temporary use of the current tab

When you open Hover Cover from the Chrome toolbar and turn it on for the current tab, the extension temporarily accesses that tab. It uses the tab's website origin (for example, the site's scheme and host) only to decide whether the cover should remain in effect after the page navigates, and it adds or removes the cover in that tab. This happens entirely on your device.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not send your image, your preferences, website origins, or any other data to the developer or to any third party.
  • It does not contain analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not use remote code; all code is contained in the extension package.
  • It does not sell or share personal information.

Removing your data

You can replace the stored cover image at any time in the extension popup. Uninstalling Hover Cover deletes the image and preferences stored locally by the extension.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
